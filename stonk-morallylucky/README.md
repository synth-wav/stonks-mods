# Morally Lucky (`stonk-morallylucky`)

> *"Where a significant aspect of what someone does depends on factors beyond his control, yet we continue to treat him in that respect as an object of moral judgment, it can be called moral luck."*
> Thomas Nagel, *Moral Luck* (1976)

**Morally Lucky** interrupts your trading career with normative ethical dilemmas: what *ought* you to do? Every option is a stance one of three theories would defend: **virtue ethics (VE)**, **consequentialism (UTL)** or **deontology (DNT)**. Every choice has an immediate effect, followed days or weeks later by a consequence that **luck** decides. The same decision can make you a hero or send you to prison. You'll never know in advance which one you're making.

- Once you're past day 10, each day has a small chance to bring a dilemma, with at least two weeks between dilemmas. Nothing appears while you're in jail, in hospital, on holiday or in a story scene.
- A dilemma opens as a normal in-game choice dialog. When it involves one of your contacts, their portrait appears with it.
- Your choice has an **immediate** effect, then a **delayed consequence** a few days to a few weeks later. Luck decides the consequence. Delayed consequences survive saving and loading.
- Money at stake scales with your wealth, so dilemmas matter just as much at 10 million as at 10 billion.
- Every choice is recorded in your **moral ledger** under VE, UTL or DNT. After every third choice, *Know Thyself* tells you which tradition you're drifting toward.
- Make **5 choices** in one school and its great figures start to seek you out: Mill, Bentham and Godwin for UTL; Kant, Ross and Socrates for DNT; Aristotle, Confucius and Marcus Aurelius for VE. Each school has one great reward, one dire ordeal, and one encounter where your choice decides. Each figure appears once per save.
- **F7** forces a dilemma, **Ctrl+F7** forces an encounter once one is unlocked, and **Shift+F7** shows your ledger.
